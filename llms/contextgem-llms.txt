# ContextGem

> ContextGem is a free, open-source framework for extracting structured data and insights from documents using LLMs and decision models: you describe what to extract as aspects and concepts, and ContextGem handles the prompts, the data models, the source references and the justifications.


2025, Shcherbak AI AS
